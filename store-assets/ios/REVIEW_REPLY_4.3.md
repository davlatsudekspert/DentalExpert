Hello,

Thank you for the review. We believe the app meets 4.3(a), 4.3(b) and 4.2.6: it is not a copy of another app, and it is not built from a template or generator. It is one of four apps we publish for four different professions, and we explain below how they differ. We answer your questions in order.

**1. What the app does and the problem it solves**
Dental Expert is a study and teaching app for dentistry students and dental teachers in Uzbekistan, in Uzbek (with Russian and English). It contains: (a) a curriculum of 15 dental subjects and 120 topics with lessons and picture-based practice; (b) a bank of about 1,250 multiple-choice questions, written and audited by us (each question has a checked answer key, explanation and distractors), used for practice, review and exams; (c) an interactive 3D tooth atlas; (d) a classroom feature: a teacher creates a group, shares a QR/code, assigns a test from the question bank (with per-student question variants, a deadline and optional general test), students answer on their phones and the teacher sees results; (e) a presentation mode so a teacher can show lesson slides on a classroom screen. Dentists can also create a profile, which our owner manually verifies, and patients can find verified dentists in their city. The main problem solved: dental students in Uzbekistan have almost no structured study material or exam practice in Uzbek, and teachers have no simple way to give and grade a test in class.

**2. Intended users**
Primary: students of dental faculties and colleges in Uzbekistan, and their teachers. Secondary: practising dentists (professional profile, continuing study) and patients looking for a verified dentist in their city. Users sign in with an email code (or Apple/Google), choose a role (student, teacher, dentist, patient).

**3. Gap in the market**
To our knowledge, general dental or medical quiz apps are in English and follow foreign curricula. This app follows the Uzbek dental curriculum in Uzbek, combines the question bank with a teacher-to-student classroom workflow (assignment, per-student variants, deadline, results) in one app, and offers a verified-dentist directory for Uzbek cities. We are not aware of an app that offers this combination in Uzbek. [CONFIRM: wording of "to our knowledge"]

We distributed builds through TestFlight (builds 20, 21 and 22 so far) and tested them on real iPhones. **5. Standalone or part of a suite**
It is part of a small suite of professional study apps by one developer (us): Dental Expert (dentistry), Forensic (forensic medicine), LabGuide (laboratory science) and NurseGuide (nursing). Each app is for a different profession and has entirely different content: its own curriculum, question bank, illustrations and terminology (Dental Expert: dental subjects, tooth atlas, dentist directory; the others: their own fields). What the apps have in common is the teaching workflow: a teacher shares a QR code, students join and take a test, the teacher sees results. We consider this a standard classroom feature, like a quiz in any education app; it is not the product. Dental Expert additionally has things the others do not: the dental curriculum (15 subjects, 120 topics), the 3D tooth atlas, picture-based practice, the presentation mode for slides, and the verified-dentist directory. **6. Could it be delivered inside another app of ours**
Not sensibly. Each app serves a different profession and student body, with its own content, questions and store listing; combining dentistry, nursing, forensics and laboratory content into one app would make it large and confusing for a student who wants only their own field, and each field needs its own review of content and its own health/education disclosures in the stores. Users of one app have no need of the others' content. [CONFIRM: these are the real reasons; edit in your own words]
