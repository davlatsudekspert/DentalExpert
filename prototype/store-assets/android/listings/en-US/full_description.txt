Dental Expert is a study and work companion for dentists, dental teachers and dental students, in Uzbek, Russian and English.

FOR DENTISTS
• Keep your own patient list with a tooth chart (odontogram, FDI numbering) and visit notes.
• The patient list is stored only on your phone. It is not uploaded to our server.
• Optionally publish a public profile so patients can find you: workplace, short bio and the contact channels you choose (phone, Telegram, Instagram, email, map link).

FOR TEACHERS AND STUDENTS
• Teachers create groups and share a join code with their students.
• Give assignments and quizzes; quizzes are scored automatically.
• Teachers see students only by the nickname they choose, never by email.

LEARN EVERY DAY
• Daily practice: a short quiz and a picture task every day. Your daily results stay on your device.
• Illustrated lessons and view-only slide presentations in Uzbek, Russian and English.
• 3D tooth atlas: rotate a tooth and explore enamel, dentin, pulp and root in cutaway views.

FIND A DENTIST
• Browse dentists by country and city. Profiles marked "Sample" are fictional demonstrations.

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
• Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with your Google account.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking or analytics SDKs.
• Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and never sold or shared with third parties.
• Delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

IMPORTANT
Dental Expert is an educational and reference tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace a consultation with a qualified dentist. This is an early version; we welcome your feedback through Settings → Send a complaint or message or davlatsudekspert@gmail.com.
