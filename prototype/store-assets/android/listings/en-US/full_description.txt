Dental Expert is an app for learning dentistry, for dental students, teachers and dentists, in Uzbek, Russian and English.

LEARN STEP BY STEP
• A sample curriculum: year → subject → topic, from propaedeutics to implantology.
• Every topic has illustrated slides, oral questions and a test.
• Picture practice, review of mistakes and a timed exam mode.
• Daily practice: a short quiz and a picture task every day. Your daily results stay on your device.

3D TOOTH ATLAS
• Rotate a tooth and explore enamel, dentin, pulp and root in cutaway views.

FOR TEACHERS AND STUDENTS
• Teachers create groups and share a join code or a QR code with their students.
• Give assignments and class tests from any topic; tests are scored automatically.
• Run a test live on the big screen while students answer on their phones.
• Teachers see students only by the nickname they choose, never by email.

FOR DENTISTS
• Refresh your knowledge with the curriculum, the lessons and the 3D atlas.

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
• Sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with your Google account.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking or analytics SDKs.
• Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and never sold or shared with third parties.
• Delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

IMPORTANT
Dental Expert is an educational and reference app. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace a consultation with a qualified dentist. This is an early version; we welcome your feedback through Settings → Send a complaint or message or davlatsudekspert@gmail.com.
