# Dental Expert - Competitor Analysis

Prepared 2026-10-17. Sources are linked inline; [S#] refers to the source list at the end. **[unverified]** marks a claim I could not confirm from a primary or reliable source. Prices change by store and region; treat them as indicative.

---

## 1. App-by-app

### Group 1 - Anatomy and education

**Complete Anatomy (Elsevier / 3D4Medical)**
- Best at: full male/female 3D models, virtual dissection, AR. Courses, videos and radiology are included in every subscription [S1]. Faculty can build lectures from screens, videos and quizzes and track engagement through Curriculum Manager (Institutional tier only) [S2].
- Loved for: depth and polish, and offline use. Downloaded content works without internet and syncs later [S3].
- Money: annual subscription (Student / Professional / Institutional) with a student first-year discount [S1]. Third-party sites list about $40-75 per year for students and about $100 for professionals [unverified].
- Copy: **teacher-built "lectures" that embed atlas views**. A teacher pins a specific tooth layer state into a slide or assignment.

**Visible Body - Human Anatomy Atlas**: one-time paid atlas ($24.99 US) with a "3D Dental Anatomy" add-on (about $7.99) [S4]; loved for no subscription. Copy: **sell the dental atlas as a nameable product** ("Dental Atlas Pro").

**eHuman 3D Tooth Atlas**
- Best at: dental-specific morphology, embryology, occlusion and caries. Its Tooth Viewer lets an instructor manipulate models in real time while students follow along [S5].
- Loved for: built for dental and hygiene curricula.
- Money: institutional and retail licence. No current price found [unverified].
- Copy: **"follow the teacher" live mode**. The teacher rotates or separates a tooth and the students' screens mirror it.

**Anatomage Table**: touchscreen table for "peeling back" layers built from real CT and Visible Human data, used in medical and dental teaching [S6][S7]; institutional hardware [price unverified]. Copy: **"real-data" provenance labels**, which suits Dental Expert's photographic atlas.

**Osmosis (Elsevier) / AMBOSS / Lecturio-type**
- Best at: Osmosis has short illustrated videos, each tied to quizzes, notes and linked resources, plus instructor analytics that flag at-risk students [S8]. AMBOSS has a library of 1,500+ topics plus a 13,900+ question bank and an Anki integration [S9].
- Money: AMBOSS charges $149 per year, or $19.99 per month, for the library with 50 questions a month; the full Qbank is $448 per year [S9]. Freemium metering of questions is the key tactic.
- Copy: **every quiz explanation links back to the exact atlas/lesson screen**, and a free tier is metered by questions per month.

**Anki / Quizlet**
- Best at: Anki offers spaced repetition. It is free on desktop, Android and web, and AnkiMobile costs $24.99 one-time [S10]. Quizlet's Learn mode is adaptive [S11].
- Loved for: proven retention and user-made decks.
- Money: Anki makes one paid app. Quizlet Plus costs about $36-48 per year and puts offline use and image uploads behind the paywall [S11, partly unverified].
- Copy: **spaced-repetition review queue for missed quiz items** ("3 cards due today").

### Group 2 - Patient-facing

**DentalMonitoring**
- Best at: patients scan at home with a smartphone and a ScanBox retractor, and AI tracks 130+ observations [S12][S13]. FDA De Novo authorisation was granted in May 2024 [S14].
- Loved for: fewer in-office visits; a clinic page cites up to 58% fewer check-ups [unverified, secondary].
- Money: B2B, paid by the practice.
- Copy: **guided photo capture flow** (framing overlay and angle checklist) for future patient-to-doctor photo sharing.

**Toothpic**
- Best at: six guided photos and a health questionnaire. A dentist annotates the photos and sends a report in under 24 hours; it is not for emergencies and cannot prescribe [S15].
- Money: distributed as a no-cost Delta Dental insurance benefit [S15].
- Copy: **clear "what this is / is not" safety copy** on any remote-advice feature.

**Oral-B app / Colgate Connect**
- Best at: zone-by-zone brushing coaching, pressure and timing feedback, and a score at the end of each session [S16]. Colgate tracks 16 zones with a 3D coach [S17].
- Loved for: gamified daily habit; data can be shared with a dentist [S16].
- Money: hardware.
- Copy: **2-minute brushing timer with a 3D zone guide driven by the tooth atlas**. No hardware needed.

**Zocdoc**
- Best at: real-time booking and reviews collected from real patients after visits [S18][S19].
- Money: practices pay a fee only when a *new* patient books, with no subscription [S18]. Third parties put it at about $35-110 per booking [unverified].
- Copy: **"verified visit" reviews**: only users with a confirmed contact or visit can review.

**Doctolib**
- Best at: booking with strong reminders, which users single out [S20]. Practitioners manage no-show status themselves [S21].
- Money: per-practitioner SaaS from about €139 per month; a free presence tier is reported [S22, third-party].
- Copy: **automatic reminder sequence** (24h and 2h) once booking exists.

**Practo (India)**
- Best at: a two-sided model. Clinics pay for SaaS, patients get a free marketplace of "verified" doctors [S23]. Practo reported profitability in FY25 [S24].
- Copy: **use the doctor-side tool to seed the patient-side marketplace.** Dentists who keep their patient list and odontogram in Dental Expert get a listing as a by-product.

**CIS / Uzbekistan local**
- DMED (booking, records, notifications, reviews; one review says only family-doctor booking works), Med24 (directory with visit reminders) and Paramedics (Tashkent diagnostics, reviews, prices) [S25]. No dental-specialist leader was found. "Doctor.uz" and "Medme" were not found [unverified].
- ProDoctorov (Russia) rejected about a quarter of submitted reviews in 2025 and puts public fake-review warnings on clinic pages [S26]. Dentistry is about 7% of its reviews [S27].
- Copy: **publish the moderation stats** and flag profiles caught gaming reviews.

**SmileDirectClub (cautionary)**: shut down abruptly on 8 Dec 2023, leaving unfulfilled orders and no support [S28]. Lesson: never let patients depend on the app for treatment continuity without disclaimers and data export.

### Group 3 - Dentist practice tools

**Dentally (UK, cloud)**
- Patient portal: medical history, e-signed treatment plans and consents, online booking with practice rules, and automated recalls [S29][S30].
- Money: per-licence SaaS (about £185-390 per licence per month per G-Cloud; other figures conflict) [S29].
- Copy: **digital medical-history form the patient fills before the visit**.

**Curve Dental**
- Anatomically drawn odontogram. Tapping a tooth shows its images and history. Perio charting covers pocket depth, bleeding, mobility and furcation, and Perio+ accepts voice dictation [S31].
- Copy: **tap-a-tooth history panel and voice entry** in the patient-list odontogram.

**Dentrix Ascend (Henry Schein One)**: cloud system with built-in reminders and a role-based daily to-do list [S32]. Copy: **"Today" dashboard for dentists**.

**Open Dental**: GPL open-source system [S33]; whether the core is free conflicts across sources [unverified]. Copy: **standard FDI/Universal numbering and procedure codes**.

**Dental Intelligence**: auto-generated "Morning Huddle" (yesterday's production, schedule gaps, balances) and Patient Finder with 27 filters [S34]. Copy: **weekly dentist digest** ("4 patients overdue for recall").

**Pearl - Second Opinion**: FDA-cleared AI marking caries, calculus and periapical lesions on X-rays; assisted readers found 36% more lesions (company figure) [S35]. Lesson: AI diagnosis is a regulated device (section 4).

### Group 4 - Classroom and habit

**Kahoot!**
- Live game-show quizzes with a free host tier; students join free ("you pay for hosts, not players") [S36].
- Paid Edu tiers are reported at about $15-25 per teacher per month and add AI question generation [S37, unverified].
- Copy: **live classroom quiz mode** with a join code and a leaderboard on the teacher's screen.

**Google Classroom**
- Rubrics (reusable and importable), originality reports, and guardian email summaries, since guardians cannot log in [S38].
- Copy: **reusable rubrics on assignments** and an optional weekly summary to the teacher.

**Duolingo streaks**
- Learners with a 7-day streak are 3.6x more likely to complete the course (correlation). Letting users hold 2 streak freezes raised daily actives by 0.38% [S39]. Over 600 streak experiments were run in four years [S40, secondary].
- Copy: **streak + streak freeze on daily quiz / daily picture**. Forgiveness matters as much as the streak.

## 3. (b) Trust and retention mechanics

**Trust**
- **Verification badges.** Show "Licence verified on <date>" and re-verify yearly. Uzbekistan has a doctors registry decree and facility licence registers [S41][S42], but I found no public per-doctor lookup [unverified], so start with manual document review.
- **Demo-data hygiene.** Label every unverified listing "Demo" and never rank it above real ones. Remove demo listings once a city has 10 or more real dentists.
- **Reviews.** Only after an accepted request or visit. Show the distribution, allow public dentist replies, and publish moderation counts. ProDoctorov does this and notes that AI-written fakes are common [S26].
- **Safety and source copy.** "Not for emergencies / not a diagnosis" on advice features [S15]. Author and last-reviewed date on education content [S8].

**Retention**
- **Streaks with freezes** (max 2, earned through activity) and weekly group goals [S39].
- **Notifications.** Few and targeted, opt-in per category: daily quiz at a chosen time, assignment due in 24h, request accepted, appointment in 24h and 2h. None by default for minors.
- **Offline use.** Downloadable packs; offline progress syncs later [S3].
- **Class and dentist loops.** Opt-in first-name class leaderboard. For dentists, a weekly digest and a profile-completeness meter, so they see value before patients arrive.

## 4. (c) Privacy and regulatory pitfalls

| Area | Rule | Affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Uzbekistan data localisation** | Law on Personal Data, Art. 27-1 (ZRU-666, in force April 2021): Uzbek citizens' personal data must be stored on servers physically in Uzbekistan and registered in the State Register. It has been enforced with throttling of major platforms, and it blocked Apple Pay and Google Pay entry in 2024 [S43][S44][S45]. | Everything: accounts, patient lists, reviews. **Highest-priority issue.** If you use Cloudflare or Supabase outside Uzbekistan, get local legal advice, or host the personal-data store in Uzbekistan. |
| **GDPR Art. 9 (EU users)** | Health data is a special category and needs explicit consent (e.g. a two-step confirmation) or another Art. 9 basis [S46]. | Odontogram, medical-history form, patient photos, and possibly reviews that reveal treatment. |
| **GDPR Art. 8 (minors)** | Digital-consent age is 13-16 depending on the member state. Below it, parental consent is needed, with "reasonable efforts" to verify it [S47]. | Student and patient sign-up. Collect a birth year at sign-up. |
| **COPPA (US)** | Under 13: verifiable parental consent. The 2025 amendments (compliance by 22 Apr 2026) need separate consent for third-party disclosure and widen "personal information" to include biometrics [S48]. | Brushing coach for children, classroom use with young pupils. |
| **Apple 5.1.1(v)** | Apps that let users create accounts must offer in-app account deletion that deletes data, not just deactivates the account [S49]. | Passwordless sign-in still counts as account creation. Must ship before App Store review. |
| **Apple 5.1.3** | Health data may not be used for advertising or data mining, or stored in iCloud. Apps giving diagnosis or treatment advice may be asked for regulatory approval [S50][S51] (current clause wording only partly verified). | The **dentist "ads" feature must never target on patients' health data.** Also applies to any AI or photo triage. |
| **Google Play Health apps declaration** | Required for all apps in the Health/Medical categories. Needs a public, non-geofenced privacy-policy URL (not a PDF) and the minimum permissions [S52][S53]. | Play Console listing. |
| **Medical-device line** | AI that reads X-rays or photos is a regulated device (e.g. Pearl's FDA clearance; DentalMonitoring's De Novo) [S35][S14]. | Do not market any AI "diagnosis". Keep to education and communication. |
| **Professional advertising** | Rules on how doctors may advertise vary by country [unverified for Uzbekistan]. | The dentist ads feature: no before/after claims or price guarantees without review. |

Practical defaults: require parental consent below 16 (the strictest EU threshold); keep health data in separately encrypted, access-logged storage; let patients export and delete their own data.

## 5. (d) 30/60/90-day roadmap

**Days 0-30: credibility and compliance**
- In-app account deletion and data export. Public privacy-policy URL. Play Health declaration. Age gate.
- Decide where Uzbek personal data is hosted and get a legal opinion.
- Verified-dentist flow v1 (manual review, badge). Tighten demo labelling.
- Streaks, a single streak freeze and opt-in daily push for the daily quiz and picture.
- Source credits on atlas and education content.
- **Exit metric:** 20+ verified dentists in Tashkent; D7 retention baseline measured for students.

**Days 31-60: learning depth**
- Spaced-repetition queue for missed items.
- Quiz explanations that deep-link into the atlas.
- Offline packs for atlas and slides.
- Teacher analytics v1 (weak topics, completion) and reusable rubrics.
- Brushing coach v1 for patients.
- Odontogram: FDI numbering and tap-a-tooth history.
- **Exit metric:** median student streak at least 4 days; 3 teacher groups active weekly.

**Days 61-90: marketplace loop**
- Request-appointment flow with reminders (24h and 2h).
- Verified-contact reviews with moderation.
- Dentist weekly digest and profile-completeness meter.
- Teacher live quiz mode (Kahoot-style). "Follow my atlas" comes next.
- Consent-gated medical-history form (only after hosting and legal decisions are made).
- Monetisation tests: metered free quizzes (AMBOSS model), a premium "Dental Atlas Pro" (Visible Body model), and a featured dentist listing (no health-data targeting).
- **Exit metric:** 100 accepted appointment requests; first 30 verified reviews; at least 1 institution pilot.
