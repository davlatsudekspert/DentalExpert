1. Who is responsible

The Service is operated by its owner, reachable at davlatsudekspert@gmail.com. Send privacy requests to that address.

In the mobile app you can sign in with the same email code; the access token is stored on your device and removed when you sign out or delete the account. The app also reads public demo content (sample clinicians, institutions, slides) from our server, which is why your IP address is visible to our hosting provider. Items you "save" stay in memory on your device only.

Groups and assignments. If you join a teacher’s group or create one, we store the group, your membership, an optional nickname you choose (never shown with your email or name), and your answers or quiz results. Teachers see members only by nickname or number. Quiz scoring happens on our server using the teacher’s key. Daily practice results stay on your device. Leaving a group or deleting your account removes this data.

Sign-in methods and public profiles. You can sign in with an email code, Google or Apple; from Google or Apple we receive only your verified email and, if you allow it, your name. Dentists can optionally add phone, workplace, specialty, address, Telegram, Instagram, a public email and a map link to their profile, and ask to be listed. Nothing is shown to patients until the app owner verifies the profile; changing these details removes the verification. Phone, workplace and bio are stored on our server with your account and deleted with it. Patient lists kept by dentists never leave the device.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no selling or renting of personal data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera or photos. A photo you pick in a profile form is previewed on your device and is not uploaded.

4. Service providers

We use Cloudflare (hosting, database, content delivery) and Resend (sending the sign-in email, which contains your email address and the code). These providers process data on our behalf and may do so outside Uzbekistan, including in the United States.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data. When signed in on the website, Settings → Delete account removes your account and related sign-in records immediately. You can also write to the contact address above. We handle requests as required by applicable law, including the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Personal Data” and, where it applies, the GDPR.

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Codes and session identifiers are stored hashed. Access to the database is limited to the service. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account and we will delete it.

8. Health information

Content in the Service is for education and general information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it has not been reviewed by a medical specialist. Always consult a qualified dentist.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and material changes will be announced in the Service.

Группы и задания. Если вы вступаете в группу преподавателя или создаёте её, мы храним группу, ваше участие, необязательный псевдоним по вашему выбору (он не показывается вместе с email или именем) и ваши ответы или результаты тестов. Преподаватель видит участников только по псевдониму или номеру. Результат теста считается на нашем сервере по ключу преподавателя. Результаты ежедневных заданий остаются на вашем устройстве. Выход из группы или удаление аккаунта удаляет эти данные.

Способы входа и публичные профили. Вход возможен по коду из email, через Google или Apple; от Google или Apple мы получаем только подтверждённый email и, с вашего разрешения, имя. Стоматологи могут по желанию добавить в профиль телефон, место работы, специальность, адрес, Telegram, Instagram, публичный email и ссылку на карту и попросить включить профиль в список. Пока владелец приложения не проверит профиль, пациентам ничего не показывается; при изменении этих данных проверка снимается. Телефон, место работы и описание хранятся на сервере вместе с аккаунтом и удаляются вместе с ним. Списки пациентов, которые ведёт стоматолог, не покидают устройство.
